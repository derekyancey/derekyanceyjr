About

I'm Derek Yancey Jr. I design brands and websites, write the words that go on them and cut the videos that sell them.

I studied film and television at Regent University and started as a staff director in Virginia Beach, shooting and editing music videos and branded campaigns.

In 2019 I started Moonbear. It makes plugins, title templates, LUTs and typefaces for editors. I've launched more than 40 products there with no sales team, and teams at Adidas, Warner Music Group, Bulgari and Vice use them. The typefaces on this page came from it.

Since then I've split my time between my own brand and in-house roles. At Chimney Cricket I ran e-commerce and online revenue grew 35% behind a site redesign. At a metabolic health group in Sarasota I started as the video and social manager and ended up running marketing.

I'm based in Lakewood Ranch, Florida. The full work history is on my LinkedIn and my NOVA page.
